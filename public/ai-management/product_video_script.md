# 🎥 Video Script: AI Management System (Under 2 Mins)

This script provides a high-impact, functional overview of the platform, structured for a 110-second demo video.

---

### **[0:00 - 0:15] Introduction: The Core Purpose**
* **Visual**: Show the modern sidebar, glowing logo, and the main dashboard loading up with sleek raising animations.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > "Welcome to the AI Management System—the ultimate control hub for tracking, managing, and optimizing your enterprise's AI resources and budgets. Let's take a quick walkthrough of how it works."

---

### **[0:15 - 0:45] Page 1: Operations Dashboard (Mission Control)**
* **Visual**: Click through the **Operations** links in the sidebar (*Overview*, *LLM Provider*, *RAG Space*, *Infrastructure*). Zoom in briefly on the redesigned split-column KPI cards and the frosted glass charts.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > "First, the **Operations Dashboard** acts as your mission control. Under **Overview**, get instant totals for spend, remaining tokens, and active LLMs. 
  > 
  > In **LLM Provider**, filter and monitor token volumes and error rates per model. 
  > 
  > Under **RAG Space**, track vector database capacity and retrieval latencies. 
  > 
  > You can also check your live server **Infrastructure** cores, organize **Teams & Agents**, and view automated **Future Spend Forecasts** to prevent budget overruns."

---

### **[0:45 - 1:15] Page 2: Resource Management**
* **Visual**: Click on the **Resource Management** section in the sidebar. Show the *Agent Inventory* list and then toggle to *Employee Admin*. Hover over the bulk action buttons at the top of the table.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > "Next is **Resource Management**, where admins control AI deployments. 
  > 
  > The **Agent Inventory** tracks the health status, environment, and ownership of every active AI chatbot in your system. 
  > 
  > In **Employee Admin**, you can select employees in bulk to allocate token balances, adjust department budgets, restrict specific models, or suspend access—giving you full governance over resource consumption."

---

### **[1:15 - 1:40] Page 3: Employee Portal (Self-Service)**
* **Visual**: Navigate to the **Employee Portal** in the sidebar. Select a test employee from the dropdown list. Show the usage charts and active agents chips.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > "Finally, the **Employee Portal** provides a transparent view for team members. 
  > 
  > Here, employees can select their profile to track their personal token limits, check their cumulative spend trend, view their most used models, and see recommendations to optimize their prompt efficiency. They can also request model access or budget extensions directly from this screen."

---

### **[1:40 - 1:50] Outro: Summary**
* **Visual**: Show the full dashboard interface with the pulsing active indicator. Fade to black.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > "An elegant, comprehensive ecosystem designed to make enterprise AI operationally efficient and fully observable. Thank you!"
